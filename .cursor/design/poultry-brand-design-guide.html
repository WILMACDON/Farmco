Brand and Design Guide

A working guide for the poultry farm inventory app. "FlockLedger" is a placeholder name; swap it for your real farm name and everything else still applies.

1. Brand

Personality: reliable, warm, plain-spoken. It feels like a well-kept farm ledger, not enterprise software. Workers use it with dirty hands in bright sun, so clarity beats decoration.

Design principles

  • Fast to record: Common entries in three taps or fewer.
  • Big and clear: Large touch targets, large numbers.
  • Never ambiguous: Status is always color plus label plus dot.
  • Trust the numbers: Show who changed what and when.

Voice

  • Short, direct sentences: "12 crates added."
  • Everyday words: "eggs", "feed", "crates".
  • Calm errors: "Only 4 crates of Large left. Reduce the amount."
  • No jargon, no exclamation marks in errors.

Logo

An egg with a green leaf, paired with the wordmark in Fraunces Bold. Keep clear space equal to the egg's width on all sides. Minimum size: 24px tall. Use the egg alone as the app icon and favicon on a Eggshell or Field Green background. Don't stretch, recolor the egg, or add shadows.

2. Color

Green means farm and "good". Yolk amber is the highlight and action color for "attention". Both work on the warm Eggshell background.

Dark mode uses a deep green-black background (#131914) with a lighter green (#6DBB7F) as primary. Text on Yolk is always Barn Brown (#3A2A00); never white text on Yolk. Body text contrast must meet WCAG AA (4.5:1).

6. Layout, spacing and icons

  • Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px. Nothing else.
  • Radius: 10px for buttons and fields, 14px for cards, full for badges.
  • Mobile first: Single column on phones with a bottom navigation bar (Dashboard, Birds, Eggs, Feed, Orders). Sidebar on desktop, with Stats and Users added.
  • Touch targets: 44px minimum, 12px gaps between them.
  • Icons: Lucide, 24px, 2px stroke, always paired with a text label in navigation.
  • Shadows: Almost none. Use 1px borders (Line color) to separate cards.
  • Charts: Green for main series, Yolk for comparison, Brick only for problems (mortality, losses). Label lines directly and avoid legends where possible.
  • Role visibility: Hide actions a role can't use (for example, no "Mark as Sold" for workers) rather than showing disabled buttons.
  • Empty states: A short friendly line and one button, e.g. "No orders yet. Add the first one."
